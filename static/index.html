How it works

From pasted game to clear lessons in three steps

No account, no setup. Bring a game and MoveLens does the rest.

Bring a game

Paste its PGN, upload a file, or type your username under the Chess.com or Lichess tab and pick a recent game.

  • Chess.com: open the game → Share → PGN → Copy.
  • Lichess: open the game → the PGN tab under the board → Copy.

Choose your view

Enter your username and the board opens from your side. Or pick White or Black yourself. Then choose how deep Stockfish should look: Fast for a quick scan, Deep for a strong review, Maximum when you want the best possible answer.

Learn from it

Step through the game with the coach, read why a move was a mistake, then play your own moves on the board and get an instant verdict. Use Retry to find the better move yourself.

Move labels

What each label means

Every move is compared with the engine's best move. The difference is measured in winning chance, the percentage chance of winning from a position, because a lost pawn matters far more in an equal game than when you are already a queen up.

A note on labels. No site publishes its exact rules, so MoveLens uses sensible, open thresholds. You may see a different label for a move than on another site, and that is normal. Accuracy uses the same open formula as Lichess.
Play on the board

Don't just read the review. Test it.

The board is live. Stop at any move, play something different, and see straight away whether it was good.

Questions

Frequently asked

Where do I find my game's PGN?
  • Chess.com: open the finished game, press Share (the arrow icon), choose the PGN tab and copy it.
  • Lichess: open the game, scroll below the board and copy from the PGN tab.
  • Or skip all that: use the Chess.com or Lichess tab above, type your username and pick a game.
Is it free? Do I need an account?

MoveLens is free and needs no account or sign-up.

How long does a review take, and what do the depths mean?

The engine searches every position of your game. A deeper search finds better moves and takes longer, roughly in proportion to the number of moves. Times below are typical for a 40-move game on a fast computer; a shared free server can be slower.

DepthSearchTypical timeBest for
Fast12 ply, ≤0.15 s≈ 10 sA quick scan
Standard16 ply, ≤0.4 s≈ 30 sEveryday review
Deep20 ply, ≤1.5 s≈ 1–2 minLearning from a game
Maximum24 ply, ≤3.5 s≈ 3–5 minImportant games
Why does a label differ from another website?

No site publishes its exact rules for labels like Brilliant or Great, and engines differ slightly in how deep they look. MoveLens measures how much winning chance a move gives away compared with the best move, then applies the thresholds listed in the Move labels section. The idea is the same, the numbers are not identical.

How is accuracy calculated?

Each move gets a score from how much winning chance it lost against the best move. The game score blends a weighted average (sharp moments count more) with a harmonic mean (which punishes any very bad move). It is the same published formula Lichess uses.

Can I view the board from Black's side?

Yes. Choose Black under "View the board from", or type your username and leave it on Auto: MoveLens finds which side you played. You can flip the board at any time with the flip button or the F key.

Can I analyse a position from the middle of a game?

Yes. Step to the move you want and play on from there. The board judges every move you make, and the Engine tab shows the best lines for any position you reach.

Who can see my games?

Your game is sent to the server so the engine can analyse it, and the finished review is held in the server's memory for a limited time. The recent reviews on this page are stored only in your own browser, and you can remove them whenever you like.
